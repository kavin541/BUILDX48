THE CHALLENGES

PROBLEM STATEMENTS

“Choose a problem. Build a solution. Make an impact.”

PROBLEM 01 AI & Education

AI-Driven Personalized Skill Gap Identification & Career Readiness

Problem statement: Build an AI-powered solution that helps students identify skill gaps, create personalized learning paths, and improve career readiness through interactive tools and analytics.

PROBLEM 02 Healthcare & Wellness

Accessible Health Information & Timely Preventive-Care Platforms

Problem statement: Develop a technology solution that helps people access healthcare information, track personal wellness metrics, or receive timely preventive-care support and alerts.

PROBLEM 03 Sustainability & Climate

Smart Environmental Resource & Carbon Reduction Systems

Problem statement: Create a solution that tackles real-world environmental challenges such as waste management, water conservation, energy efficiency, or carbon reduction in communities.

PROBLEM 04 Smart Cities & Communities

Civic Engagement & Modern Urban Mobility Infrastructure

Problem statement: Build a solution that makes cities and communities smarter by improving transportation, public services, safety, accessibility, or civic engagement for citizens.

PROBLEM 05 Open Innovation

Unrestricted Tech Prototype for Real-World Impact

Problem statement: Identify a meaningful real-world problem and build an innovative technology prototype using AI, web, mobile, cloud, IoT, or other emerging technologies.
